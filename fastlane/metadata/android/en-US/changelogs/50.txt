Two data-loss defects found by audit and settled on a device.

* an edit made while its own upload was in flight is no longer acknowledged by
  that upload: contacts guard on the provider's version counter, calendars on an
  in-flight marker, and a row that moved stays pending for the next run
* a contact's photo is no longer replaced on the server by an edit that only
  touched something else: the "unchanged" baseline is a digest of the photo's
  bytes, which the act of storing it cannot invalidate
