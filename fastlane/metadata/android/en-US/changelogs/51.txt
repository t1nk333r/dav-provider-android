Upgrading from an earlier version no longer costs a contact its photo on the
server.

The baseline that decides whether a photo changed moved onto the raw contact in
0.7.0, so contacts synced before that arrived with none -- and no baseline means
"changed", which would have replaced the server's photo with a re-encode on the
first edit to each of them. A clean contact without a baseline is now given one
before anything is uploaded.
