Fixes found by an independent review of the write-back path.

* a conflict on a recurring event could send the phone's rejected edit to the
  server without a version check, overwriting the server's copy
* an edit refused by a read-only Collection could stay on the phone looking
  synced, on servers that report changes incrementally
* switching two Collections quickly, or while "Check collections" ran, could
  undo one of the switches
* the Collection screen closed at once after a device restore
* photo baselines are adopted in batches, so large address books finish
